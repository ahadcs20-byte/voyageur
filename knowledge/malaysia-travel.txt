Title: ACCESSIBILITY GUIDE: Malaysia for Travelers with Disabilities
Content:
=== ACCESSIBLE TRANSPORT ===
Kuala Lumpur:
- MRT Sungai Buloh-Kajang Line: All 31 stations have elevators, tactile paving, and wheelchair spaces
- LRT Kelana Jaya Line: Select stations have elevators, check station map
- Monorail: Limited accessibility (stairs at some stations)
- Rapid KL Bus: Low floor buses available on select routes
- GrabPlus: Wheelchair-accessible vehicles via Grab app (additional RM10-20)
- KLIA Ekspres: Wheelchair accessible with staff assistance
Penang:
- Rapid Penang Bus: Some low floor buses, call ahead to confirm
- CAT Free Shuttle: Limited accessibility
- Grab: Available, call driver to confirm accessibility needs
Langkawi:
- Car Rental: Can arrange with hand controls (advance booking required)
- Grab: Limited availability
- Private Tours: Many operators offer accessible tour packages
=== ACCESSIBLE ATTRACTIONS ===
Kuala Lumpur:
1. KLCC Park - Paved paths, accessible restrooms, free
2. Petronas Twin Towers - Elevators, ramps, accessible restrooms
3. Aquaria KLCC - Flat walkways, wheelchair available for rent
4. Islamic Arts Museum - Ramps, wide spaces, elevators throughout
5. National Museum - Ramps, elevators, accessible restrooms
6. Pavilion Kuala Lumpur - Fully accessible shopping mall
7. Suria KLCC - Fully accessible shopping mall
8. Sunway Lagoon - Accessible rides, wheelchair rental available
Penang:
1. Penang Botanic Gardens - Paved paths, benches throughout
2. Kek Lok Si Temple - Inclinator available, some stairs
3. Gurney Drive Hawker Centre - Ground level, wide walkways
4. Penang Hill - New funicular trains with wheelchair spaces
5. Entopia Butterfly Farm - Paved paths, accessible throughout
Langkawi:
1. Langkawi Sky Bridge - Accessible via cable car, bridge has slopes

Title: MALAYSIAN CUISINE: Complete Food Guide by Category
Content:
=== MALAY CUISINE ===
1. Nasi Lemak
Description: Coconut rice with sambal, fried chicken, egg, peanuts, cucumber
Where to Eat: Village Park (KL), Wanjo (KL), local mamak shops
Budget: RM8-15
Best Time: Breakfast, any meal
Halal: Yes
2. Nasi Kandar
Description: Steamed rice with variety of curries and side dishes
Where to Eat: Line Clear (Penang), Pelita (KL), Kayu (KL)
Budget: RM10-20
Best Time: Lunch, dinner
Halal: Yes
3. Satay
Description: Grilled meat skewers with peanut sauce, ketupat
Where to Eat: Kajang Satay, Jalan Alor (KL), local night markets
Budget: RM1-1.50 per stick
Best Time: Evening, dinner
4. Rendang
Description: Slow-cooked dry beef curry with coconut and spices
Where to Eat: Malay restaurants, authentic nasi padang
Budget: RM10-25
Halal: Yes
=== CHINESE CUISINE ===
1. Char Kuey Teow
Description: Stir-fried flat rice noodles with prawns, cockles, egg, bean sprouts
Where to Eat: Gurney Drive (Penang), Jalan Alor (KL)
Budget: RM8-15
Best in Penang
Non-halal (contains pork lard)
2. Hokkien Mee
Description: Dark soy sauce fried noodles with pork and squid
Where to Eat: Jalan Hokkien Mee (Penang), Taman Paramount (KL)
Budget: RM10-18
Best for: Dinner
3. Bak Kut Teh
Description: Herbal pork rib soup served with rice and youtiao
Where to Eat: Klang (origin), Jalan Ipoh (KL), Sungai Way
Budget: RM15-25 per person
Best for: Breakfast, lunch
Note: Non-halal
4. Dim Sum
Description: Steamed dumplings, buns, and small plates
Where to Eat: Dolly Dim Sum (KL), Jin Xuan (KL)
Budget: RM20-40 per person
Best Time: Morning, lunch
5. Wantan Mee
Description: Egg noodles with BBQ pork, dumplings, and vegetables
Where to Eat: Local kopitiam, hawker centers
Budget: RM7-12
Best for: Breakfast, lunch
=== INDIAN CUISINE ===
1. Banana Leaf Rice
Description: Rice served on banana leaf with curries and vegetables
Where to Eat: Sri Paandi (KL), Devi's Corner (Bangsar)
Budget: RM10-20
Vegetarian options available
Halal: Most are not halal (unless specified)
2. Roti Canai
Description: Flaky flatbread with dhal curry
Where to Eat: Any mamak shop
Budget: RM2-5
Best for: Breakfast, supper (24h)
Halal: Yes
3. Nasi Briyani
Description: Spiced rice with chicken, mutton, or fish
Where to Eat: Briyani King (KL), local Indian restaurants
Budget: RM12-25
Halal: Most are halal
4. Tandoori Chicken
Description: Yogurt-marinated chicken baked in clay oven
Where to Eat: Indian restaurants
Budget: RM15-25
Halal: Most are halal

=== DESSERTS & SWEETS ===
1. Cendol
Description: Shaved ice with green rice flour jelly, coconut milk, palm sugar
Where to Eat: Penang Road Famous Cendol, local stalls
Budget: RM3-5
2. ABC (Ais Batu Campur)
Description: Shaved ice with red beans, jelly, corn, syrup, condensed milk
Where to Eat: Any hawker center, mamak
Budget: RM3-6
3. Durian
Description: King of fruits, creamy custard-like flesh
Where to Eat: Jalan Alor (KL), Balik Pulau (Penang)
Season: June-August
Budget: RM20-100 per fruit
Note: Strong smell, not allowed in hotels
4. Kuih
Description: Traditional Malay cakes and desserts
Where to Eat: Morning markets, pasar malam
Budget: RM1-3 per piece
Varieties: Kuih Lapis, Onde-onde, Seri Muka
